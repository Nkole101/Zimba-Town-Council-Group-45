Welcome to Zimba District

Zimba is one of the fifteen districts that make up Zambia’s Southern Province. Situated approximately 410 kilometres southeast of Lusaka along the Great North Road (T1).The district stands as a strategic and vibrant gateway connecting communities within Zambia and beyond.

it shares boundaries with four other districts in the province namely, Kalomo to the north, Kazungula to the southwest, and Choma and Sinazongwe to the east. To the south, Zimba enjoys an international border with Zimbabwe, separated by the majestic Zambezi River. The “Keep Zambia Clean, Green, and Healthy” campaign is a government-led program that aims to improve the health of the country by keeping the environment clean and green. The campaign involves a variety of activities, including: 

  • Cleaning: Weekly cleanings in key areas, such as the Central Business District 
  • Raising awareness: Educating residents and traders on the importance of keeping the environment clean and the negative effects of improper waste disposal 
  • Community involvement: Encouraging the public to participate in the campaign 
  • Reporting: Encouraging people to report those who dump garbage in unauthorized places to law enforcement.
