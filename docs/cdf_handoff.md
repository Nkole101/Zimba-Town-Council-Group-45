# Handoff status — 11 September 2026

The review described below is the extraction stage. Subsequent Part 1 cleaning is implemented in `scripts/clean_cdf.py` and the executed `cdf_cleaning.ipynb`, including literal pandas detection, IQR and export cells. Current final files include `description_clean` / `body_text_clean`, and missing project statuses are `unspecified`. See `docs/cdf_cleaning/README.md` for the cleaning handoff.

The final pipe-delimited CSV contains 44 reviewed project/announcement records from 19 council news posts, dated 2 November 2023 to 27 April 2026. The public WordPress `?rest_route=/wp/v2/posts` endpoint reported one page containing 50 published posts; all 29 CDF keyword candidates were fetched successfully and their HTML retained. Funding amounts are blank in 33 rows, status in four rows, and constituency/ward in two rows because the source was missing or ambiguous. HTTPS verification was explicitly disabled for this collection, robots.txt returned 404, and page requests were delayed by at least one second.

Run `python scripts/build_cdf_reviewed.py` to reproduce the reviewed CSV offline from `raw/cdf_projects/archive_snapshot.json`. Explicit review decisions are recorded in that script. The automatic parser's 42 unreviewed candidates are preserved in `raw/cdf_projects/candidate_projects.csv`, and `data/cdf_source_posts.csv` retains cleaned text from the 29 candidate posts. Running the scraper again overwrites the final path with automatic candidates; the review builder restores this fixed snapshot, but new announcements require fresh review.

Rows are announcements, not a unique-project register. Contract awards, completion reports and later monitoring are retained when separately reported. Do not sum funding amounts across rows as total spending: Treasure Compound appears in two same-day announcements. Bundles remain together where a defensible split is unavailable; clearly named classroom and health projects are separated.

The 2026 approvals headline claims eleven projects, but its body identifies ten named packages used here; no eleventh was invented. The Mapatizya Clinic article contradicts itself over mothers' shelter funding, so only the unambiguous maternity annex is retained. Muzya Road's K3.2 million is a wider allocation, not a confirmed road-specific cost, so its amount is blank; the malformed loan figure `K2, 93, 400` is also blank. Publication dates are used even when articles describe earlier events. Ward spellings remain as reported; the mixed constituency column follows the assignment and may hold a constituency, ward or locality. Completed for loans/grants refers to disbursement/award, not repayment or beneficiary outcomes.

Ten keyword candidates were excluded as general performance, contractor advice, budgets, national policy, training, tree-planting guidance without explicit CDF funding, a general council-meeting report without explicit attribution of road funding, or donor-funded sanitation. The Lifalale Farmer Field School example from the assignment was excluded because its source does not identify CDF funding. Coverage is limited to published API news, not PDFs, unpublished projects or a complete asset register.

Validation checked the ten-column pipe-delimited schema, unique sequential IDs, ISO dates, permitted statuses, positive amounts, source URLs and saved HTML for every row. Five parser tests pass. The administration dataset is unchanged.
